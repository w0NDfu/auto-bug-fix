# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Auto-Bug-Fix welcoming and
respectful for everyone, regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and focus feedback on
ideas and code rather than people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately harmful or
deceptive conduct are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's GitHub
contact channels. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where
possible.
